Questions

What does it cost?

The cost is the return you offer plus any fees charged by the platform. The calculator shows the cost of the loan and the APR for your choices. .

How much can I borrow?

The platform sets your approved amount based on your profile, and new borrowers may start smaller. We fund up to that amount only when funds are available.

What if you cannot fund my amount?

You can join the waitlist. We contact you when funds become available. Joining does not guarantee funding.

What if I cannot repay on time?

Contact us before your due date. Late payments may carry fees and can affect your standing. .

Is approval guaranteed?

No. The platform decides approval, and funding depends on available funds.

What do I need to start?

Only your name, phone number and email. We send instructions for the next steps.
