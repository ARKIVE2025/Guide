Weekly or monthly tuition, registration fees and deposits that come due before your next paycheck.

After-school programs

Summer and camps

What it costs, including late fees

No surprises. Here is every cost to know before you apply.

The return you offer

A percentage of your loan paid to the lender, up to 15%. The calculator shows this cost before you apply.

Platform fees

The third-party platform may charge its own fees. They are shown in the platform's documents.

Late fees will apply

If you repay after your due date, late fees will apply. They are set and charged by the third-party platform, and they are shown in your loan documents.

Estimated funding dates

Dates apply only when funds are available. Your repay-by date counts from your funding date.

Borrow with a plan

Short-term funding works best when you know exactly how and when you will repay.

  • Choose an amount you can repay by your due date, including the return.
  • Check the repay-by date and total cost before you apply.
  • If you may be late, contact us before the due date. Late fees will apply after it.
  • Approval and funding are decided by the third-party platform and are never guaranteed.

Questions

What does it cost?

The cost is the return you offer plus any fees charged by the platform. The calculator shows the cost of the loan for your choices. .

How much can I borrow?

The platform sets your approved amount based on your profile, and new borrowers may start smaller. We fund up to that amount only when funds are available.

What if you cannot fund my amount?

You can join the waitlist. We contact you when funds become available. Joining does not guarantee funding.

Can I use this for daycare or after-school costs?

Many families use short-term funding for childcare tuition, registration fees and after-school program costs. Borrow only what you can repay by your due date.

Is this only for parents?

No. We are focused on families right now, but anyone with a short-term need can check availability.

What if I cannot repay on time?

Contact us before your due date. Late fees will apply after it, and late payments can affect your standing. .

Is approval guaranteed?

No. The platform decides approval, and funding depends on available funds.

What do I need to start?

Only your name, phone number and email. We send instructions for the next steps.
