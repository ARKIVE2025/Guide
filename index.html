Questions

How much can I borrow?

Your approved amount is set by a third-party platform based on your profile, and new borrowers may start with a smaller amount. We fund up to what you are approved for, based on the funds available in each cycle.

When will I be funded?

On our cycle dates. After you are approved, you are funded on the next available date in the schedule above.

How long do I have to repay?

You choose 14 or 28 days from your funding date. Your exact due date is shown in your loan documents.

What is the return to lender?

It is an amount you choose to offer the lender, from 0% to 15% of the amount. You see the exact dollar amount before you accept. .

What if I cannot repay on time?

Contact us before your due date. Late payments may carry fees and can affect your standing with the platform. .

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Approval is decided by the third-party platform, and funding depends on cycle availability.

What do I need to request funding?

Only your name, phone number and email. We send documents and instructions for the next steps.
